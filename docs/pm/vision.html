Vision & Goals

Why MoMoney exists and what it is trying to achieve

The Problem

Most personal finance apps share a common set of painful trade-offs. They either require too much manual work, provide poor categorization, or demand that you hand over sensitive financial data to a third party — often all three.

❌ What's Broken Today

  • Manual expense entry is tedious and unsustainable
  • Auto-categorization accuracy is often poor
  • Limited long-term analytics and historical comparisons
  • Poor data portability — you're locked into the app
  • Financial data stored without explicit user consent
  • No ability to split & attribute expenses to household members

✅ How MoMoney Solves It

  • Automated transaction ingestion via statement upload
  • Rules-based + merchant-dictionary categorization
  • Monthly, yearly, and custom-range analytics
  • Full data portability: SQLite, CSV, JSON
  • Privacy-first: no data stored without explicit opt-in
  • Owner assignment & expense splitting for families/couples

Product Vision

Mission: Build a privacy-first personal finance platform that enables users to import bank statements, automatically categorize transactions, analyze spending patterns, and maintain full ownership and portability of their financial data.

The system is delivered as a web application, but the backend is API-first so that future mobile apps can be built on the same foundation without a re-architecture.

Primary Goals

⚡

Reduce Friction

No manual entry. Upload a statement file and let the system do the rest — parsing, deduplication, and categorization happen automatically.

🎯

Accurate Categorization

Combine merchant normalization with a configurable rules engine to categorize transactions correctly from day one and improve with use.

💡

Clear Spending Insights

Give users a true picture of where their money goes — by category, merchant, time period, and household member.

👨‍👩‍👧

Household Attribution

Assign owners to transactions and split expenses across people. Generate per-person breakdowns inside monthly invoices.

📂

Full Data Portability

Users can export their data as SQLite, CSV, or JSON at any time. No lock-in.

🔐

Privacy as Default

Financial data is never persisted by default. Cloud storage is an explicit opt-in with clear consent prompts.

📈

Long-term Growth Tracking

Track net worth over months and years. Visualize how assets and liabilities move over time.

📱

API-First Architecture

The backend is designed to support web and future mobile clients without re-architecting the data layer.

What's Out of Scope (v1)

To maintain focus and ship a high-quality v1, the following capabilities are explicitly deferred:

🚫 Not in v1

  • Direct bank API integrations (no Open Banking / account aggregation)
  • Bill payment functionality
  • Tax preparation or filing
  • Investment trading or portfolio management
  • Financial advisory or AI-driven recommendations (scoped for future)
  • Automatic recurring transaction detection
