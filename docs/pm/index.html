What is MoMoney?

MoMoney is a web-based personal finance application built for individuals and families who want clear, automated insights into their spending — without sacrificing privacy. Users upload their bank statements; MoMoney handles everything else: parsing, categorization, analysis, and reporting.

📥

Upload Statements

Upload PDF or CSV bank statements. MoMoney automatically parses transactions and presents a clean preview before import.
