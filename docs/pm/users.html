Target Users

Who MoMoney is built for and the jobs it does for them

Primary User

🧑‍💻

The Cost-Aware Individual

An individual who wants to understand their monthly spending without the burden of manually logging every purchase. They have bank accounts and credit cards, receive monthly statements, and want clear insights — but don't want to trust a third-party app with their financial data indefinitely.

  • Uses digital banking (ICICI, HDFC, Axis, SBI, etc.)
  • Comfortable uploading a downloaded statement PDF or CSV
  • Values having control over their own data
  • Interested in monthly spending summaries and category breakdowns

Secondary Users

👫

Couples

A couple managing shared finances. They want to understand combined spend, see who spent what on joint expenses, and split costs fairly each month.

Key feature: Owner assignment + invoice analysis

🏡

Families

Families with multiple earners or household members. They want to assign transactions to individuals and generate a clear monthly household statement.

Key feature: Household owners + expense splits

🔒

Privacy-Focused Users

Users who are deeply uncomfortable giving any third-party cloud access to their bank data. They want powerful analytics but with zero server-side persistence.

Key feature: Session Mode + local SQLite export

📚

Finance Enthusiasts

People who track their net worth, categorize every transaction, and build detailed spending trend reports. They want depth and configurability.

Key feature: Rules engine + historical insights + net worth tracking

User Jobs To Be Done

When I… I want to… So that…
Get my monthly bank statement Upload it and immediately see categorized transactions I understand where my money went without manual entry
See an Amazon transaction Categorize it as Shopping, Electronics, or Groceries My spending reports are accurate
Pay for a shared dinner Split the transaction between myself and my partner I know exactly what each person actually spent
End of the month Generate an invoice summarizing all tagged transactions I have a permanent record and can settle shared costs
Reviewing yearly finances See a month-by-month spend trend by category I can identify lifestyle inflation and cut unnecessary spend
Concerned about cloud storage Use the app without any data being saved to a server My bank data stays completely private
Planning for financial goals Track my net worth over time I can measure financial progress month over month
