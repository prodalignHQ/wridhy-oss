📊 Insights & Analytics

Understanding where your money goes — monthly, yearly, and over time

Overview

MoMoney's Insights module gives users a multi-dimensional view of their spending. From a quick monthly summary to long-term category trends, all analytics are derived directly from the user's imported transactions — no manual entry required.

Monthly Spend Dashboard

The primary analytics view. Gives a clear snapshot of what happened in a given month.

💸

Total Spend

Sum of all debit transactions in the selected month, with a delta vs. the previous month.

🧩

Category Breakdown

Donut or bar chart showing total spend per category. Click into any category for transaction-level detail.

🏪

Top Merchants

Leaderboard of highest-spend merchants for the month, with transaction count and total.

📅

Spend Timeline

Day-by-day or week-by-week spend within the month — useful for spotting spikes or unusual dates.

Historical Insights

Analyze spending patterns across time to identify trends, growth, and lifestyle changes.

Supported Time Ranges

  • Monthly view
  • Yearly view
  • Last 12 months
  • Last X years
  • Custom date range

What You Can Analyze

  • Category trends over time
  • Top merchant trends
  • Per-owner spend comparison
  • Spending growth/decline
  • Month-over-month δ for any category

Merchant Analytics

Merchant-level analytics reveal how much you spend with any individual merchant and across which categories.

Example: Amazon Merchant View

  • Total spend: ₹1,24,000
  • Transactions: 63
  • Categories split: Shopping (52%), Electronics (30%), Groceries (18%)

This helps users understand that Amazon isn't just one category — it's a mix of different spending types that may need to be tracked separately.
