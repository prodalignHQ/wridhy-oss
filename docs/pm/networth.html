📈 Net Worth Tracking

Measure and visualize your total financial position over time

Overview

The Net Worth module allows users to track their total financial position — assets minus liabilities — month over month. This gives a macro view of financial health that complements the transaction-level spending insights.

Formula: Net Worth = Total Assets − Total Liabilities

How It Works

Users log their current assets and liabilities. The system calculates a net worth snapshot and stores it with a date. Over time, these snapshots form a timeline that can be visualized as a growth chart.

Draft Support

In-Progress Snapshots

The system maintains a single "draft" per user — the in-progress working state for an upcoming net worth snapshot. Users can update assets and liabilities over several sessions before committing the snapshot. When they're ready, they save it as a permanent monthly entry.
