🧾 Invoices

Monthly financial summaries — showing who spent what, across all accounts

What is an Invoice?

An invoice in MoMoney is a consolidated monthly spending report. It pulls together transactions from one or more bank statements (across different accounts and banks) that were all tagged to the same month. Once generated, it produces a permanent snapshot with a detailed owner-level spending breakdown.

Key use case: A couple uploads their ICICI savings and HDFC credit card statements for March 2025. They create an invoice combining both. The invoice shows total household spend, category breakdown, and who (Alex vs. Sam) spent how much.

Invoice States

📝 Draft

A work-in-progress invoice. Drafts can be reopened and updated at any time. They do not lock the underlying transactions. Multiple changes to categories and owner splits can be made before generating.

EditableNot permanent

✅ Generated

A finalized invoice. A snapshot of all transaction data (amounts, categories, owner splits) is stored at generation time. Even if underlying transactions are later modified, the invoice snapshot is preserved.

Permanent snapshotRead-only

Invoice Analysis

After generating an invoice, the Analysis view provides two key breakdowns:

👤 Who Spent How Much?

Total debit spend per owner, calculated using the assigned split amounts on each transaction.

  • Alex: ₹24,000
  • Sam: ₹18,500
  • Household (shared): ₹12,000

📊 Who Spent Where?

Category-level breakdown per owner. Respects owner split amounts — not just transaction totals.

  • Alex → Food & Dining: ₹8,400
  • Alex → Transport: ₹3,200
  • Sam → Shopping: ₹11,000
Note: Only debit transactions are included in the spending analysis. Credit transactions (income, refunds) are visible in the invoice but excluded from owner spend calculations.

Multi-Account Invoices

Combining Multiple Statements

An invoice can include statements from multiple accounts and multiple banks — as long as they are all tagged to the same month.

  • ICICI Savings — March 2025 ✓
  • HDFC Credit Card — March 2025 ✓
  • Axis Wallet — March 2025 ✓

All three may be combined into a single "March 2025 Invoice" to get a complete picture of household spending across all sources.
